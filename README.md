# 🍕 Pizza Place Sales Analysis — 2015

## Project Overview
Analysis of a full year of sales data from a fictitious pizza place using MySQL 
and Power BI. The goal was to answer key business questions and build an 
interactive dashboard to support decision-making.

## Tools Used
- **MySQL** — data cleaning and querying
- **Power BI** — interactive dashboard

## Business Questions Answered
1. What are the peak hours and days for orders?
2. How much total revenue was generated in 2015?
3. Which month had the highest and lowest revenue?
4. What are the top 5 bestselling pizzas?
5. Which pizza category generates the most revenue?

## Key Insights
- 🕛 Peak order hours are **12:00–13:00** and **17:00–19:00**
- 📅 Busiest days are **Thursday, Friday and Saturday**
- 💰 Total revenue for 2015 was **$817,860.05** across **21,350 orders**
- 📈 **July** was the highest revenue month at **$72,557.90**
- 🍕 **The Classic Deluxe Pizza** was the bestselling pizza with **2,453 units**
- 📊 **Classic** was the top revenue category at **$220,053**
- ⚠️ **The Brie Carre Pizza** is the lowest performer — recommended for a 
promotional strategy rather than removal
